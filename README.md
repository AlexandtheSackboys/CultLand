# The Cult Culler



The Cult Culler is first-person, action, horror-themed, wave-based shooter set in the English countryside, where a farmer needs to protect his land from a cult that want to take it over.



"It's time for a unique harvest... of livestock!"



What if the shooting gameplay and Ridiculous weapons of Call of Duty Zombies mixed with the setting of Clarkson's Farm and the enemies of Hot Fuzz!



===========



**Installation Instructions**



* Open Itch.io
* Search for "The Cult Culler" in the search tab
* Click on the game and locate the button which says download
* 



===========



**Gameplay Instructions**



* Start the game once you are familiar with the controls
* You will be spawned into the farm level at wave 1, where your goal is to eliminate all of the cultists in each wave to earn points and unlock new locations across the map
* Once you acquire \_ points, you will be able to purchase 1 of 5 wacky weapons this game has to offer
* Cultists that are eliminated have a 20% chance to drop an apple which can be picked up by walking over it to heal any damage taken



===========



**Controls**



* WASD keys to move around
* Space bar to jump
* Left Shift to sprint
* Left mouse button to shoot
* R to reload
* Tab to switch weapons
* ESC to pause



===========



**Wacky Weapons**



5 wacky weapons to use in game, each with their own unique twist



* Ice Staff
* Harpoon Gun
* Hornet Rifle
* Wild Revolver
* Vortex Cannon
